Privacy Policy

Crowd Rush: Run & Multiply

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Who we are

Crowd Rush: Run & Multiply ("the game") is a free mobile game published on Google Play by Aonix Games (package name com.aonixgames.crowdrush), which is the data controller for the purposes described here.

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It contains advertising and optional in-app purchases.

2. Data stored on your device

The game does not require an account. The following is saved only in the app's private storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • game progress: unlocked levels, stars, coins, boosters, cosmetics, best crowds and records, lives and timers;
  • settings: sound, music, vibration;
  • your privacy choices and flags for purchases you own (for example Remove Ads), so they keep working offline.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes this information. Purchases are managed by Google Play and can be restored through the game's store screen.

3. Advertising

The game is supported by ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay mediation. The ad networks currently enabled are Unity Ads and ironSource Ads.

On first launch the game asks you to choose how ads may use your data. The advertising SDK is initialised only after you make that choice. When ads are shown, the ad networks may collect and process:

  • your Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be inferred;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • ad interactions such as impressions, clicks and video completions.

This data is used to deliver ads, measure their performance, limit how often the same ad is shown, prevent fraud, and — only if you chose personalized ads — to select ads based on your interests. If you choose non-personalized ads, ads still appear, but they are not tailored to you; limited data may still be used for measurement and fraud prevention.

Ad formats used in the game are interstitial ads shown at natural breaks and optional rewarded videos, which only play when you choose to watch them.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing (through Unity In-App Purchasing). Google handles your payment details; we receive only confirmation that a purchase completed and which product was bought. We never see your card or bank information. Google keeps purchase records under its own privacy policy.

5. What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise GPS location. The game includes no account system, no cloud save, and no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own. We do not sell your personal information for money.

6. Your choices

  • In the game: open Settings › Privacy & Ads at any time to switch between personalized and non-personalized ads, or to turn on "Do not sell/share my info (US)" (CCPA/CPRA).
  • On your device: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Remove Ads purchase: buying Remove Ads stops interstitial ads. Optional rewarded videos remain available, because they only play when you ask for one.
  • Changing your choice does not remove ads by itself; it changes how your data may be used for them.

7. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalized advertising and for accessing your advertising ID for that purpose;
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for non-personalized advertising, measuring ads, fraud prevention and keeping the game secure;
  • Contract and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)) for processing purchases and keeping the records we are required to keep.

You may withdraw consent at any time using the in-game option above; this does not affect processing that already took place.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and provided information to an ad network, contact us and we will help you address it, including by putting you in touch with the relevant provider.

9. Retention and deletion

We do not operate servers and do not hold personal data about you. Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data processed by Unity and Google is retained according to their own policies; you can contact them directly for access or deletion requests. For any other request, write to us at the address below.

10. Security

Data sent by the advertising and billing SDKs is transmitted over encrypted connections where the provider supports it. The game stores no passwords or accounts, and local data sits in the app's private storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Third-party services

These providers process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, ironSource Ads): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google (Google Play, Google Play services): policies.google.com/privacy

If we enable additional ad networks through LevelPlay in the future, this page will be updated to name them.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Where a change affects how your data is used, the game will ask for your consent again before continuing to serve personalized ads.
